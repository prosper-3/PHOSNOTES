PHOSNOTES POLICIES

Refund & Digital Products Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund & Digital Products Policy explains how purchases of digital products from PHOSNOTES ("PHOSNOTES", "we", "us", or "our") are handled.

PHOSNOTES currently focuses on digital children's books and related educational resources.

2. Digital Products

PHOSNOTES products are primarily digital products, such as ebooks, educational activities, worksheets, and other downloadable resources.

The format of each product will be identified on the relevant product page or during the purchasing process.

Digital products may be supplied in formats such as PDF or another electronic format appropriate for the product.

3. Digital Product Purchases

Before completing a purchase, customers should review the product description, intended age range, format, file type, and other information provided on the product page.

By completing a purchase, you acknowledge that you are purchasing a digital product rather than a physical book unless the product description specifically states otherwise.

4. Digital Delivery

After a successful purchase, the digital product may be delivered through a download link, email, customer account, digital delivery platform, or another method specified during checkout.

Delivery times may depend on the payment provider and digital delivery service being used.

If you have completed payment but cannot access your purchased product, please contact PHOSNOTES so that we can investigate the issue.

5. Technical Problems

If a purchased file is corrupted, incomplete, inaccessible, or technically unusable because of an issue with the file or our delivery system, please contact us.

Where appropriate, we may provide a replacement file, a new download link, restored access, or another reasonable solution.

Customers should provide enough information for us to identify the purchase and understand the technical problem.

6. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same digital product more than once, please contact us as soon as possible.

We may review duplicate transactions and, where appropriate, provide a refund or another suitable resolution.

7. Product Does Not Match Its Description

If you believe that a digital product materially differs from the description provided at the time of purchase, contact PHOSNOTES and explain the issue.

We will review the issue and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include providing a corrected product, replacement access, or a refund where appropriate.

8. Change of Mind

Because digital products may be delivered and accessed immediately after purchase, refunds for change of mind may not be available once a digital product has been successfully delivered or downloaded, where applicable law permits this.

This does not limit any mandatory consumer rights that may apply to your purchase.

9. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a purchase was made without your authorization, contact PHOSNOTES as soon as possible.

We may request information necessary to investigate the transaction and may also work with the relevant payment provider where appropriate.

Customers should also promptly notify their bank or payment provider if they believe their payment credentials have been compromised.

10. Refund Requests

To request assistance with a purchase, contact us using the email address below.

Please include, where available:

  • Your name
  • The email address used for the purchase
  • The name of the digital product
  • The approximate date of purchase
  • Your order or transaction reference
  • A description of the problem

Providing this information can help us investigate your request more quickly.

11. Payment Providers

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may have their own terms, policies, security procedures, and dispute processes.

PHOSNOTES does not store complete payment card details when payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider.

12. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you experience a problem with a purchase, we encourage you to contact PHOSNOTES first so that we can attempt to resolve the issue.

This does not prevent you from exercising any rights available to you through your payment provider, bank, or applicable law.

13. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove, restrict, or override consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or limited.

Where mandatory consumer protection laws provide rights that differ from this policy, those legal rights will apply to the extent required by law.

14. Misuse of Digital Products

A refund request does not grant permission to continue distributing, copying, reselling, or publicly sharing a PHOSNOTES digital product.

Purchased digital products remain subject to the intellectual-property and usage restrictions described in our Terms & Conditions .

15. Changes to This Policy

PHOSNOTES may update this policy when our products, payment systems, delivery methods, or legal obligations change.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

If you have a question about a purchase, digital delivery, technical problem, or refund request, please contact PHOSNOTES.
