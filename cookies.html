Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how PHOSNOTES ("PHOSNOTES", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website.

PHOSNOTES is a children's publishing brand focused on digital children's books and educational resources.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit the website. They allow a website to remember certain information about a visit or support particular technical functions.

Similar technologies may include other methods used to store or access information on a device or to understand how a website is being used.

3. Cookies Currently Used by PHOSNOTES

At the current stage of the PHOSNOTES website, we do not intentionally operate advertising, behavioral-tracking, or marketing cookies on the website.

The current website is primarily a static website consisting of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Its JavaScript provides functions such as navigation, smooth scrolling, visual effects, and other basic website interactions.

We therefore do not claim that the current PHOSNOTES website uses Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, or similar marketing technologies unless those services are actually added to the website.

4. Essential Website Technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for a website or its hosting infrastructure to operate securely and reliably.

These technologies may be provided by PHOSNOTES or by infrastructure providers used to host, secure, or deliver the website.

Such technologies are different from optional advertising or marketing trackers because their purpose is to support the operation, security, and delivery of the website.

5. Analytics

PHOSNOTES may introduce analytics tools in the future to understand website traffic and improve the website.

If we introduce an analytics service that uses cookies or similar technologies, we will update this Cookie Policy to identify the relevant service, explain its purpose, and describe the applicable choices available to visitors.

6. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

PHOSNOTES does not currently use advertising cookies or marketing tracking technologies on the website.

If this changes in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated before or when the relevant technologies are introduced, as applicable.

7. Third-Party Services

As PHOSNOTES develops its digital publishing business, certain services may be provided by third-party companies.

These services may include payment processing, digital product delivery, website hosting, security, analytics, email delivery, or other technical services.

A third-party service may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies. Where such technologies are relevant to your use of the PHOSNOTES website or purchasing process, we will provide appropriate information about them.

8. Payment and Digital Product Services

PHOSNOTES may use third-party payment and digital delivery providers when digital products become available for purchase through the website.

Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services.

Such technologies are controlled by the relevant provider and may be subject to that provider's privacy policy and cookie policy.

9. How You Can Control Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through the browser's settings.

Browser controls may allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block certain or all cookies.
  • Receive warnings before cookies are stored, depending on your browser.

Because browser settings differ between browsers and devices, you should consult the documentation for the browser you use.
