1. Introduction

PHOSNOTES ("PHOSNOTES", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and purchasing our digital ebooks.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it, and the rights available to you.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with PHOSNOTES, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Billing or transaction information
  • Information you provide when contacting us
  • Information necessary to provide purchased digital products
  • Technical information about your device or browser where collected by our website, hosting provider, analytics services, or other technical providers

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Visit the PHOSNOTES website
  • Contact us
  • Purchase a PHOSNOTES ebook
  • Request customer support
  • Subscribe to communications, where available

4. Why We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Provide access to purchased ebooks
  • Process and manage transactions
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support

  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security problems
  • Send communications where you have requested or consented to receive them

5. Payment Information

PHOSNOTES may use third-party payment providers to process purchases.

Where a third-party payment provider processes your payment, that provider may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

PHOSNOTES does not intend to store complete payment-card information on its own website.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party services to operate our website, process payments, deliver digital products, provide hosting, analyze website traffic, or communicate with customers.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf or independently according to the services they provide.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PHOSNOTES may use cookies or similar technologies for essential website functionality, security, analytics, or other purposes.

For more information, please see our Cookie Policy .

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including fulfilling purchases, providing support, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, restriction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed.

You may also have the right to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Rwanda's personal data protection framework provides data subjects with rights concerning access to and control over their personal data.

11. Children's Privacy

PHOSNOTES creates books intended for children, but our website and purchasing process are intended to be used by parents, guardians, teachers, schools, and other adults.

We do not intentionally request personal information directly from children for the purpose of purchasing our products.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of internet services and digital products.

12. International Data Transfers

Some service providers used by PHOSNOTES may process information in countries outside Rwanda.

Where personal information is transferred or processed internationally, we will take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection requirements.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, legal requirements, or data-processing practices.
